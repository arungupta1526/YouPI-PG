## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-18

### Features
- Self-hosted sovereign UPI payment gateway with zero aggregator fees.
- Automated banking observers (SMS parsing & Gmail API integration).
- WhatsApp Berry Protocol pre-checkout OTP customer authentication.
- Pure vector SVG dynamic single-use QR generation.
- Invariant single-slot FIFO queue for UPI collision prevention.
- Authoritative HMAC-SHA256 server-to-server webhooks with raw-body signature verification.
- Offline cashier counter payments (Cash & Card at Till).
- White-label merchant customizer and supervisor control center.
